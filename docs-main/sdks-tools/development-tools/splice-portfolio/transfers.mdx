---
title: "Transfers"
description: "Send, withdraw, accept, and reject transfer offers in Splice Portfolio."
---

All transfers are sent and received through your primary party. To act from a different party, first [switch your primary party](/sdks-tools/development-tools/splice-portfolio/switch-parties).

## Send a Transfer Offer

1. From the Dashboard or your primary party view, click **Transfer** in the top right corner of the screen.
   ![Transfer button on the Dashboard](/images/SEND-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-\(STEP-1A\).png)
   <img
     src="images/SEND-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-1B).png"
     alt="Transfer button on the primary party view"
     lightAlt="Transfer button on the primary party view"
     darkAlt="Transfer button on the primary party view"
     className="dark:hidden"
   />
   <img
     src="/images/SEND-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-1B).png"
     alt="Transfer button on the primary party view"
     lightAlt="Transfer button on the primary party view"
     darkAlt="Transfer button on the primary party view"
     className="hidden dark:block"
   />
2. Complete the transfer form and select **Make Transfer**.
   <img
     src="/images/SEND-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-2)-1.png"
     alt="Completed transfer form"
     lightAlt="Completed transfer form"
     darkAlt="Completed transfer form"
   />
   The **Description** field is where any memo tag or ID received from an exchange should be pasted.
   <Note>
     If the recipient party has pre-approved the asset being transferred, an offer expiration date is not required.
   </Note>
3. Review the transfer details on the confirmation screen.
   <img
     src="images/SEND-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-3).png"
     alt="Transfer confirmation screen"
     lightAlt="Transfer confirmation screen"
     darkAlt="Transfer confirmation screen"

     alt="Transfer confirmation screen"
     lightAlt="Transfer confirmation screen"
     darkAlt="Transfer confirmation screen"
     className="hidden dark:block"
   />
4. To complete the transfer, go to your CIP-103 compliant wallet to complete signing.
5. Verify the transfer in **Offers > Transfers**.
   <img
     src="images/SEND-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-5).png"
     alt="Transfer listed with Sent status in the Offers page"
     lightAlt="Transfer listed with Sent status in the Offers page"

     className="dark:hidden"

   <img
     src="/images/SEND-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-5).png"
     alt="Transfer listed with Sent status in the Offers page"
     lightAlt="Transfer listed with Sent status in the Offers page"
     darkAlt="Transfer listed with Sent status in the Offers page"
     className="hidden dark:block"
   />

<Note>
  If the recipient party has pre-approved the asset, the completed transfer appears in the transaction history as soon as it completes on-chain.
</Note>

## Withdraw a Transfer Offer

You can withdraw a transfer offer you have sent only if the recipient has not yet accepted it and has not set up pre-approval for the asset being transferred.

1. Go to **Offers > Transfers**.
2. Select the offer you want to withdraw.
   <img
     src="images/WITHDRAW-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-2).png"
     alt="Transfer offer selected in the Offers page"
     lightAlt="Transfer offer selected in the Offers page"
     darkAlt="Transfer offer selected in the Offers page"
     className="dark:hidden"
   />
   <img
     src="/images/WITHDRAW-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-2).png"
     alt="Transfer offer selected in the Offers page"
     lightAlt="Transfer offer selected in the Offers page"
     darkAlt="Transfer offer selected in the Offers page"
     className="hidden dark:block"
   />
3. Click **Withdraw**.
   <img
     src="images/WITHDRAW-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-3).png"
     alt="Withdraw button on a transfer offer"
     lightAlt="Withdraw button on a transfer offer"
     darkAlt="Withdraw button on a transfer offer"
     className="dark:hidden"
   />
   <img
     src="/images/WITHDRAW-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-3).png"
     alt="Withdraw button on a transfer offer"
     lightAlt="Withdraw button on a transfer offer"
     darkAlt="Withdraw button on a transfer offer"
     className="hidden dark:block"
   />
4. To complete this action, go to your CIP-103 wallet to complete signing.

## Accept or Reject a Transfer Offer

There are two ways to respond to a transfer offer.

1. From the Dashboard, select the transfer offer.
   ![Incoming transfer offer on the Dashboard](/images/ACCEPT-OR-REJECT-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-\(STEP-1A\).png)
   Or navigate to **Offers > Transfers** and select the transfer offer.
   <img
     src="images/ACCEPT-OR-REJECT-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-1B).png"
     alt="Transfer offer detail in the Offers page"
     lightAlt="Transfer offer detail in the Offers page"
     darkAlt="Transfer offer detail in the Offers page"
     className="dark:hidden"
   />
   <img
     src="/images/ACCEPT-OR-REJECT-A-TRANSFER-OFFER-(STEP-1B).png"
     alt="Transfer offer detail in the Offers page"
     lightAlt="Transfer offer detail in the Offers page"
     darkAlt="Transfer offer detail in the Offers page"
     className="hidden dark:block"
   />
2. Click **Accept** or **Reject**.
   ![Accept and Reject buttons on a transfer offer](/images/sdks-tools/splice-portfolio/transfer-accept-reject.png)
3. To complete this action, go to your CIP-103 wallet to complete signing.
4. If accepted, review the completed transaction in your primary party's transaction history.
