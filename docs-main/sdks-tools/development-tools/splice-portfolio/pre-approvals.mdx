---
title: "Enable Pre-Approvals"
description: "Pre-authorize incoming transfers of an asset so they are accepted automatically."
---

Enabling pre-approvals for an asset pre-authorizes incoming transfer offers to your primary party. Once enabled, any incoming transfer of a pre-approved asset is automatically accepted into your primary party, with no signing required.

## Enable a Pre-Approval

1. Go to the **Settings** page and navigate to the **Pre-Approvals** section.

2. Toggle on pre-approval for the asset of your choosing.

   ![Pre-Approvals section of the Settings page with asset toggles](/images/ENABLE-PRE-APPROVALS-\(STEP-2\).png)

3. To complete this action, go to your CIP-103 wallet to complete signing.

If the asset you want to enable pre-approval for is not listed in the Pre-Approvals table, you first need to [add the registry](/sdks-tools/development-tools/splice-portfolio/add-a-registry) associated with that asset. Once the registry is added, the asset appears in the table.

<Note>
Pre-approval contracts only apply to the primary party. See [Switch Parties](/sdks-tools/development-tools/splice-portfolio/switch-parties) to change which party is primary.
</Note>
