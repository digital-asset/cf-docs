<Note>
This feature is available on DevNet only.
</Note>

The faucet lets you receive test tokens for deployment and testing purposes.

## Tap the Faucet

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the DevNet section">
Go to the **Settings** page, navigate to the **DevNet** section, and click **Tap**.

![DevNet section of the Settings page with the Tap button](/images/DEVNET-TAP-TO-GET-CANTON-COIN-\(STEP-1\).png)
</Step>

<Step title="Choose an instrument and amount">
Select the instrument type, enter an amount, then click **Tap**.

![Faucet form for selecting an instrument type and amount](/images/sdks-tools/splice-portfolio/devnet-tap-form.png)
</Step>

<Step title="Sign in your wallet">
To complete this action, go to your CIP-103 wallet to complete signing.
</Step>
</Steps>
